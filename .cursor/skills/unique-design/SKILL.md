# Unique Design — SOLARIS CET

Full: `docs/planning/unique-design.md`

## Design DNA (3 helixes)

1. **Helix Solis** — light as data (golden hour gradients = confidence scores)
2. **Helix Field** — topography lines = survey grid, not decoration
3. **Helix Trust** — AHJ/legal weight via typography, not clipart

## Laws

- Reuse Tailwind tokens + `cn()` — no orphan styles
- Motion = meaning (sync pulse = live twin, not bounce for fun)
- Every screen answers: *where on the roof is the truth?*

## Anti-generic

Reject: purple AI gradients, generic dashboard cards, stock solar clip art.
